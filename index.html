03 — Build Your Season Plan

Idea → Strategy → Build → HYPERDRIVE

Turning ideas into strategy, strategy into a build, and our build into a robot ready to compete.

Game Challenge Brainstorming

Our first step was breaking down the HYPERDRIVE challenge as a full team — mapping scoring elements, the field layout, and where a small, well-drilled team can get an edge over bigger rosters.

  • Prioritize a reliable gripper/arm mechanism over a flashy but fragile one
  • Build a consistent autonomous routine for early-match scoring
  • Keep the drivetrain light and fast for quick repositioning

Robot Build Plan

1

Design

Sketching the chassis and gripper in CAD before cutting anything.

2

Prototype

Testing the gripper and drivetrain with kit parts before committing to a final build.

3

Build

Full assembly, wiring, and mounting of electronics on the chassis — our current focus.

4

Test & Iterate

Drive practice, code tuning, and fixing what breaks before it breaks in a match.

5

Compete

Scrimmages and final checks to be match-ready for HYPERDRIVE.
